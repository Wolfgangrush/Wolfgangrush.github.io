AI Brain — enterprise editions

8 · private

The same brain turned into a multi-user server that a firm runs inside its own cloud tenant — CPU-only, no GPU, model inference rented per token. The commercial moat is not the model; it is the governance layer wrapped around it.

LayerWhat it does
SSO / OIDCAuthenticates against the firm's own identity provider — Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace. No personal API keys anywhere in the system.
Matter-level RBACNeed-to-know access per matter, with ethical walls that override even an administrator. Privilege is protected structurally, not by policy.
Cocoon lockdownOn prompt injection, credential stuffing or tamper, the server seals itself and refuses every call until an out-of-band break-glass unlock.
Tamper-evident auditAppend-only, hash-chained ledger of every invocation: who, what, when, which matter, which jurisdiction.
Jurisdiction engineResolves applicable law and data-residency per deployment, with a conservative default for anything unlisted.
DeploymentDocker Compose or Helm with TLS; ISO 27001 / 42001 audit-ready, shipped with the commercial legal pack (MSA, DPA, EULA).

Vidhi Likhit — the law, written

3 editions · private

An agentic Indian law school that runs in a terminal. It ships the full body of Indian bare acts — 11,715 acts: 899 Central and 10,816 State and Union Territory instruments across 36 jurisdictions — and turns them into a taught classroom. A dean routes each question to the right teacher, every answer is grounded in the actual section, and the student is examined on what they claimed to learn.

The corpus and the search index stay on the machine: embeddings are built on-device with ONNX, so the index is never sent anywhere. Only the conversational tutor calls out — over an engine and key the user supplies themselves.

🔒 vidhi-likhit-solo
The student edition — a faculty of 21 agents, the growth-plant progress model, notes and grading held locally.
🔒 vidhi-likhit-enterprise-full
The in-tenant edition — the same faculty plus SSO-bound turns, RBAC, cocoon, injection firewall and a hash-chained audit of every turn.
🔒 vidhi-likhit-university-edition
For law schools — multi-tenant, with cohorts, enrolment, examinations, gradebook and faculty analytics behind an education ethical wall built for exam integrity and record confidentiality.

The faculty

Vidur — the dean, who routes the question. Fifteen domain teachers — one per branch of law. Parakh — pulls the real citation. Pariksha — the examiner. Smriti — memory and notes. Mali — the gardener, who grows the learning plant as topics are mastered. Rakshak — the guardian, who refuses what should be refused.

The deterministic engines

5 · private

The part of the family that deliberately contains no model at all. Each engine encodes a legal test — not the text of a law, but the structure that decides whether a case satisfies it. Extraction is plain parsing; the rule lives in code. The verdict is therefore auditable, reproducible, and cannot be hallucinated.

🔒 nyaya-check
Cause-of-action checker. Give it the facts; it walks the statutory test, ticks each ingredient satisfied, names the limb that is missing, says who bears the burden, and gives the cure path. Built out on s.138 NI Act with a CPC / BNSS / BSA kernel.
🔒 pramaan
Electronic-evidence integrity. Fingerprints an exhibit at the moment of capture, keeps an unforgeable chain of custody, and prints the s.63 BSA certificate with the hash already filled in. Entirely offline — no model ever reads the evidence.
🔒 vidhi-delta
Citation-verification firewall for a draft about to be filed. Finds every authority asserted, catches the ones that cannot exist — a fabricated reporter, a court too young, a repealed Code cited as live — and refuses to pass the rest as verified until they have actually been read. Outputs a clean List of Authorities.
🔒 asthir
Precedent-instability radar. A citator tells you a case was distinguished; this tells you a proposition is unsettled — per incuriam, a contrary High Court, a reference to a larger bench — and whether the wobbling line is the one that binds your bench.
🔒 pratipaksha
Opposing-counsel red team for any Indian legal document. Names the ground, prayer or clause that will be attacked, the counter-authority that will be cited, and the defensive fix — strictly read-only, and it never invents a citation.

Setu — the bridge between a client and their advocate

1 · private · MVP

A tenant-isolated client portal for a practice. Clients get instant answers to the routine questions — grounded strictly in that firm's own vetted knowledge — and anything touching the client's specific facts is escalated to the human lawyer before it can do harm. The system is built so it cannot give advice; containment is structural, not a disclaimer at the bottom of the page. Every interaction lands in a tamper-evident log: what was asked, what was answered, that it escalated, that the boundary was shown.

🔒 setu
Working vertical slice, built and tested against synthetic data. Pilot deployment — real corpus, resident hosting and firm authentication — is the next milestone.

How it is built

The four commitments
CommitmentHow it is enforced
The file stays on the machineCorpus, index and matter data are local by default. Where a model is called, a jurisdiction-aware pseudonymisation gateway sits on the egress path. Enterprise deployments run inside the customer's own tenant.
The law lives in code, not in the modelStatutory tests, limitation periods, ingredient checks and citation validity are deterministic engines. A model may draft prose; it never decides whether a limb of the test is met.
Nothing passes unverifiedEvery drafting pipeline runs an anti-hallucination stage between draft and refinement, and an opposing-counsel stage before output. Citations are checked against reporter, court and vintage before a List of Authorities is signed.
The AI is a tool, never the recordEnterprise editions keep a hash-chained, append-only audit of every turn. The output is a document the practitioner owns, signs and is answerable for.
